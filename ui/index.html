How to read this

Cards show each sensor right now. Motion means its score is above the 0.06 threshold, Quiet means below it, and Silent means nothing has arrived for 15 seconds, so the sensor or the gateway may be down. Tap a card to hide that sensor on the chart.

On the chart, touch or hover to read the values. Pinch or drag sideways with the mouse to zoom, and ⟲ Live brings back the live view. The strips under the chart mark the stretches of motion, one row per sensor.

The vertical axis is motion_score, the variance of CSI amplitude over a window. Within a bucket the max is taken, so a spike is not smeared out by averaging. In bar mode the height is measured from zero and every sensor gets its own slot inside the bucket; in line mode the dots mark the real samples. Log keeps the quiet background visible next to a large spike.

The past comes from storage: the selected window is loaded from the server's history, and new points arrive live. Storage runs a few minutes behind the live stream, so if the connection drops, the hole fills in a little later.
